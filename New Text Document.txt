🏆 The Idea: AI Bali Trip Planner + Trusted Booking Platform
A website (+ WhatsApp bot) that creates personalized Bali itineraries and lets tourists book vetted services at transparent, fair prices — killing the #1 tourist anxiety: "Am I getting scammed?"
Why This Is The One
The problem is universal and emotional. The most common frustrations tourists face in Bali are taxi drivers refusing meters or quoting inflated prices, fake tour offers, currency exchange tricks, and accommodation scams. Bali Holiday Secrets From the moment you arrive in Denpasar, the issues are obvious: lengthy traffic delays, beaches marred by plastic, and at every turn someone trying to overcharge you. Tourism Review
Every single tourist going to Bali goes through the same painful research loop: hours on Reddit, TikTok, Facebook groups, and blogs asking the same questions — "How much should a driver to Ubud cost?" "Is this tour legit?" "What area should I stay in?" "What are the new rules for 2026?"
Nobody owns this. Klook and GetYourGuide are generic global platforms — they list activities but don't solve the trust problem or the planning overwhelm. There's no Bali-native, trust-first platform that says "here's exactly what things cost, here's your personalized plan, and here's how to book without getting ripped off."
What You Build (Dead Simple MVP)
A website with 3 core features:
1. AI Trip Planner (the traffic magnet — FREE)
Tourist enters: travel dates, budget, interests (surf, wellness, culture, party, family), and group size. The AI generates a day-by-day personalized itinerary with fair price estimates for everything. This is what gets shared on social media and ranks on Google.
2. Fair Price Guide (the trust builder — FREE)
A simple, always-updated database showing what things should cost:

This content is insanely shareable. Every traveler screenshots this and sends it to friends going to Bali.
3. Trusted Booking (the money maker — COMMISSION)
From the itinerary, users can book directly:

Airport transfers (vetted drivers, fixed price)
Day tours with English-speaking driver-guides
Activities & experiences (waterfall tours, temple visits, cooking classes, snorkeling)
eSIM cards (affiliate)
Scooter rental from legit providers

You take 15-20% commission on every booking. The driver/provider gets guaranteed customers without paying for ads. The tourist gets a fair price with trust.
Why It Prints Money
Let's do the math conservatively:

Bali had 7.05 million foreign arrivals in 2025 Travel And Tour World
Average tourist books 2-3 services during their trip (airport transfer + 1-2 day tours)
Average booking value: ~$30 USD
Your commission: ~$5 per booking

If you capture just 0.5% of tourists (35,000 people) booking an average of 2 services each:

70,000 bookings × $5 = $350,000/year

Scale to 2% and you're over $1.4M/year. And this is before eSIM affiliate revenue, featured listings, or premium itinerary upgrades.

Why It's Easy to Build
This is a Next.js website + WhatsApp bot. That's it.

Week 1-2: Build the website with the AI trip planner (Claude/GPT API + a curated Bali knowledge base)
Week 3: Add the fair price guide (static content, SEO goldmine)
Week 4: Add booking forms that connect to vetted drivers/providers via WhatsApp or a simple dashboard
Week 5-6: Launch WhatsApp bot version (tourists message a number, get their itinerary + booking links)

No mobile app needed. No payment processing to build initially (drivers can collect cash or you use Xendit for Indonesian payments later). No complex marketplace infrastructure.
Go-to-Market (Free / Almost Free)
This is the best part — your marketing basically does itself:
SEO content machine: Write 50 articles targeting searches tourists already make: "Bali 7 day itinerary 2026," "how much should a driver cost in Bali," "Bali scams to avoid," "best things to do in Ubud." These keywords have massive search volume and low-quality competition (mostly outdated blog posts).
TikTok/Instagram Reels: Short videos like "What things ACTUALLY cost in Bali vs what they'll charge you" — this format goes viral routinely. Bali travel content is one of the most-viewed niches on TikTok.
Reddit/Facebook seeding: Answer questions in r/bali, Bali travel Facebook groups, and link to your free trip planner. This is where tourists already go for advice.
WhatsApp virality: When a tourist uses your bot and has a great experience, they share the number with their friends. Australians alone account for over 1.6 million visits to Bali per year Road Genius — a single happy Aussie traveler shares with their entire friend group going to Bali next.
